Privacy policy

CasinoCompass calculates casino distances and directions on your device. The app has no developer-operated backend, accounts, analytics SDKs, advertising, or tracking.

Location and compass heading

When you choose live mode and grant permission, the app uses your device location and compass heading to calculate straight-line distances and direction to venues in a bundled dataset. The app does not send these sensor readings to the developer or save location history.

Demo mode uses a fixed Vancouver coordinate and simulated heading. Choosing demo stops live location and heading updates. The app also stops its updates when it enters the background.

Preferences stored on your phone

The app saves whether you acknowledged the age notice and whether venue names should be displayed. These preferences use Apple's local UserDefaults storage. They are not sent to the developer. Deleting the app removes its local app data; device backup and restore are managed by Apple and your device settings.

Maps, links, and sharing

When you open Apple Maps or Google Maps, the app passes the selected venue's coordinates (and its name for Apple Maps) to that service. The maps service may obtain your own location separately under its permissions and privacy policy.

When you tap Share, the app prepares an image and text containing the displayed distance, selected venue name if names are visible, demo status when applicable, venue notices, and a link to this website. Content is sent to another app or recipient only when you choose a sharing action. Distances and venue choices can reveal information about your whereabouts; review the card before sending it.

External websites, map apps, and sharing destinations have their own privacy practices. CasinoCompass does not control those services.

This website and support

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. This website adds no analytics scripts, advertising, forms, or browser storage. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses to operate and secure its hosting service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you contact us through GitHub Issues, your report and GitHub profile are public. Do not include precise personal locations, private documents, credentials, or other sensitive information. We use the information you choose to provide to respond to your request and improve the app.

Adults only

CasinoCompass is intended for adults who meet the legal age in their location. The age acknowledgement is a self-declaration; the app does not collect a birth date or identity document.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, use the project's support issue form without including sensitive details, or visit Support. We will update this page and its effective date when the app's privacy practices change.
